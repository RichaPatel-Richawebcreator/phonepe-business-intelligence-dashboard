## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard for digital payment transaction analytics**, designed to transform transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard analyzes:

- Transaction performance
- Transaction value
- Customer behavior
- Age-segment contribution
- Service-wise performance
- Payment success rate
- Monthly transaction trends
- Weekday vs. weekend usage
- Top users by transaction value

The primary objective is to demonstrate how a Data Analyst can move beyond data visualization and use data to answer **business questions, identify opportunities, and recommend actionable decisions**.

> **Note:** This is a portfolio/educational project. It should not be interpreted as an official PhonePe internal business report or as using confidential PhonePe data.

# 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand digital payment behavior and identify opportunities for improving:

- Transaction performance
- Customer engagement
- Service performance
- Payment success
- Business growth

### Key Business Questions

1. How is transaction volume changing over time?
2. What is the total transaction value?
3. What is the payment success rate?
4. Which services contribute the highest transaction value?

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

## 1. Transaction Trend

Transaction activity varies across months, with identifiable peaks and lower-activity periods.

This indicates that customer transaction behavior is not consistent throughout the year and may be influenced by seasonality, customer activity, campaigns, or service demand.

### Business Opportunity

Identify the drivers behind peak periods and introduce targeted campaigns during lower-activity periods to improve transaction consistency.

---

## 2. Customer Age Segmentation

The analysis shows different levels of contribution across customer age segments.

### Business Opportunity

The business can use customer segmentation to develop targeted engagement strategies instead of applying the same strategy to every customer.

Potential strategies include:

- Personalized offers
- Cashback campaigns
- Loyalty programs
- Financial-product recommendations
- Customer re-engagement campaigns

---

## 3. Service Performance

The service analysis shows that **Loans contribute the highest transaction value** among the analyzed services.

### Business Opportunity

The Loans category can be analyzed further using metrics such as:

- Conversion rate
- Average transaction value
- Repeat users
- Customer acquisition
- Funnel performance
- Drop-off rate

This can help determine the factors driving high-value transactions.

---

## 4. Payment Success Rate

The dashboard reports an overall successful transaction rate of approximately **95.93%**.

Although this represents a strong success rate, even a small percentage of unsuccessful transactions can represent a significant number of failed customer journeys at high transaction volumes.

### Business Opportunity

Failed transactions should be analyzed by:

- Service
- Month
- Customer segment
- Payment method
- Time period
- Failure reason

Reducing payment failures can improve customer experience and transaction completion.

---

## 5. Weekday vs. Weekend Usage

The analysis indicates higher transaction activity during weekdays compared with weekends.

### Business Opportunity

The business can investigate the reasons behind lower weekend activity and test targeted weekend engagement strategies such as:

- Cashback offers
- Merchant discounts
- Entertainment offers
- Travel-related offers
- Personalized notifications
- Weekend bill-payment campaigns

The results can then be measured using transaction volume, transaction value, and customer engagement.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Increase Low-Activity Period Performance

Identify months with below-average transaction activity and run targeted campaigns during those periods.

### 2. Improve Customer Engagement

Use customer segmentation and transaction behavior to create personalized engagement strategies.

### 3. Optimize High-Value Services

Perform deeper analysis of high-value services to understand customer conversion, repeat usage, and transaction behavior.

### 4. Reduce Failed Transactions

Monitor failed transactions and identify the major operational and customer-related factors contributing to failures.

### 5. Increase Weekend Engagement

Test targeted weekend campaigns and compare transaction performance before and after the campaigns.
